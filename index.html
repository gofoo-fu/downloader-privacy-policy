Privacy Policy

App: Video Downloader (视频下载器) · Package: com.gofoo.videodownloader · Effective and last updated: October 12, 2026

Summary: Video Downloader processes links you choose to paste or share, retrieves media from the relevant website, and saves downloads on your device. We do not operate a media-upload server, sell your personal information, or include advertising or analytics SDKs. Websites you access and Google Play may process information under their own policies.

1. Who we are

GoFoo (“we”) provides the Video Downloader Android application (the “App”). This policy describes the information the App handles and the choices available to you. Contact us at gofoofu@gmail.com.

2. Links, media, and network requests

When you paste or share a link and request parsing or downloading, the App sends requests directly from your device to the selected website and, where needed, its media hosts or redirect services. Those sites may receive the URL, your device IP address, request headers, and any cookies you chose to import for that platform. Their own privacy policies govern how they handle those requests. The App displays metadata such as titles, thumbnails, available formats, and image addresses returned by the site. We do not upload your downloaded media or your pasted links to a GoFoo server.

The extraction engine is included in the installed App. This version does not download or update extraction code from within the App; engine changes are distributed with App updates. The App uses third-party extraction and media-processing libraries; see its in-app open-source notices.

3. Information stored on your device

  • Downloaded video, audio, and image files; download history and export status.
  • App settings, selected language, a local free-use start time, and purchase entitlement state.
  • Platform cookie files that you explicitly import. They are kept in the App's private, excluded-from-backup storage and can be deleted in Settings.
  • A salted password verifier for the private album. The password itself is not stored in plain text.

Private downloads are encrypted on the device using a key held by Android Keystore. Encrypted .vdl files can appear in the system Downloads folder, but a file manager cannot play them directly. The App decrypts them temporarily after password verification so you can play or export them. Exported or shared copies are controlled by the destination app and may be accessible outside Video Downloader.

4. Purchases and free use

The App offers seven days of free downloading from first use. There is no automatic payment when this period ends. You may choose a one-time purchase to unlock continued downloading. Google Play Billing handles the transaction and payment details. The App receives signed purchase data, purchase tokens, status, and product information needed to verify and grant access; this version checks purchase signatures on the device and does not send purchase tokens to a GoFoo server. We do not receive your full payment-card number. The exact price is shown by Google Play before you confirm. Google's processing is described in Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Permissions

  • Internet: parse links and download permitted media.
  • Foreground service, notifications, and wake lock: continue a download you started and display its progress.
  • Storage on Android 8 and 9: write files to the system Downloads folder when requested. Newer Android versions use system-managed storage APIs.

The App does not request access to contacts, microphone, camera, or precise location.

6. Sharing, retention, and deletion

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. Your App records and local files remain on your device until you delete them, clear App data, or uninstall the App. Deleting a download inside the App deletes its stored file and record. Copies you previously exported or shared must be deleted separately at their destination. Uninstalling or clearing App data destroys the Android Keystore key, so any encrypted .vdl files left in Downloads cannot be recovered. Google Play retains purchase records under its own policies.

7. Your choices

On first launch, the App displays a summary of data handling and permitted use, with links to this policy and the Terms of Use. You may agree and continue or decline and exit. The App does not start its Google Play purchase connection before you accept. Your acceptance version and time are stored only on your device. Merely opening the App does not parse links or download media. Opening a policy link launches your browser, which contacts the policy website under its own privacy settings.

You decide which links to process, whether to import or remove cookies, whether a download is private, and whether to share or export a file. You can delete App downloads from the download lists and remove imported cookies in Settings. Because GoFoo does not operate an account or media-storage server for this App, we generally cannot access or delete files stored only on your device. For privacy questions or requests, email us.
